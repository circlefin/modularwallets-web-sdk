Create Circle Smart Account

This demo lets you register a passkey to create an MSCA wallet on Arc Testnet. It uses toCircleSmartAccount to derive and show the wallet address, and sendUserOperation (with encodeTransfer for USDC) to send 1 USDC to an EVM address of your choice. Gas is covered by the paymaster.
